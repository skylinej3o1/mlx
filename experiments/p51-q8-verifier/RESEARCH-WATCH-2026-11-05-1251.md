### FRESH / major numerical quality mechanism — 2x RTX5070Ti Blackwell MXFP4 W4A8, NOT QFN 3-bit

[llama.cpp #31743](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/pull/31743), created **17:44:03Z**, last updated17:50:49Z, **OPEN**. Proposed Blackwell \`mxf8f6f4\` MMA computes **MXFP4 weights with E4M3/UE8M0 block-scaled activations** (W4A8) instead of default FP4 activation (W4A4); this is **activation compute precision**, not a newly higher-fidelity 3bpw weight artifact. Tested on **2×RTX5070Ti** with imatrix MXFP4 Qwen3.8-27B **dense**, Qwen3.6-35B-A3B MoE and small0.8B, BF16 logit oracle, WikiText2 72×4096 windows; five speed repeats at 4096 prompt/128 decode. 27B BF16 KL **0.1875 W4A4→0.0898 W4A8→0.0860 q8 activations**; BF16 top1 agreement **83.958→90.153→90.476%**; PP4096 **2533.43→2340.93→2178.46**, TG128 **79.75→79.67→79.88**. 35B KL **0.1693→0.0683→0.0658**, top1 **82.628→89.308→89.548**, PP **5771.72→5699.58→5249.46**, TG **247.12→247.27→247.11**. Great evidence that more precise runtime activations can recover substantial numerical fidelity at modest PP cost and almost unchanged TG **for these models and dual cards**; zero proof of original QFN GSQ IQ3S or single 5070Ti coding success. The imatrix in this report is for separate MXFP4 source models, not automatically applicable to source QFN GGUF. Counterpass: benefits could be due to activation rounding alone, not improved weight quantization; no task-grade code benchmarks.
