## Checkpoint lineage and expected inference impact
A model from architecture Qwen4 may be newly pretrained, further post-trained or architecture-adjusted. Alibaba “Qwen4 currently in training” strongly supports ongoing generation training **somewhere**; it doesn't identify Flash checkpoint lineage. If Qwen4-Flash retains the 125B/51B layout and tensors, Paperniuk DS4/Metal/Basalt/GDN-QSA/PLE optimization ideas may port with version/template/logit checks; higher quant sensitivity, new vocab/PLE, revised MTP and different experts could invalidate any drop-in assumption.

## Owner M1 Max and Relay feasibility (hypotheticals, NOT specs)
- Owner has two **separate** 2021 M1 Max 32GPU64GB domains. Historic original **GSQ IQ3_XXS** physically128K PP278/TG34.2 and259K PP258/TG31.7, near57GiB residency at259K. A similarly sized 125B+51B Qwen4-Flash could plausibly fit only at very aggressive precision and careful cache/memory, NOT guaranteed; even modest growth may push beyond single-Mac comfort. A 4bit 180B theoretical raw weight floor is ~90 decimal GB **before metadata/activations** so Q4 cannot all-reside in one64GB.
- Small 35B-A3B (if released) ~20–25GB 4bit weight footprint, +context/vision/MTP, comfortable on one M1 at moderate contexts. Community Whittle 35B/10B ngram 21.3GB Q4 file is example only; official checkpoint may allocate differently. User's preferred coding quality must still pass held-out task suite.
- 27B dense Q5 maybe ~19–23GB weights and useful room on M1. 27B tier's actual architecture / checkpoint unknown, and *not equivalent to Flash*.
- Two M1s don't form coherent128GB; large Flash requires model partition + communications/PLE strategy rather than assuming united residency. CUDA RTX5070Ti16+64GB could prefill for one/two Macs through Relay ONLY if numerical QSA/GDN/PLE and MTP state compatible. Faster prefill does not solve too-large decode weights or poor coding-task quality.
