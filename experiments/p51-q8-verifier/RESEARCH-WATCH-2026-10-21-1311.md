## Executive decision — new experimental implementation lane; canonical goals unchanged

**Add a source-equivalent \`paperniuk/ds4\` single-M1Max64 / DASLab general GSQ-RCO IQ3_S / 262,144 active-token capacity experiment**, separate from the canonical two-M1 40TG/425PP 128K and 35TG/400PP 262K lanes. The near-term engineering path is **Q8 KV fidelity control -> native sparse-QSA KVarN K4V3 + 1024-token high-precision tail -> allocation audit -> shallow expert spill only if needed -> selective SSD KV spill only as a fallback for genuinely insufficient capacity or multiagent**. K4V2 is a secondary quality/risk arm. The stage must preserve exact indexer block selection/positions/pending ring, 36 GDN state, MTP rollbacks, PLE/n-gram identity and all immutable source/root hashes. There is **NO shipped ds4 K4V3 Qwen/active-KV-streaming feature, no measured M1Max IQ3_S filled262K, no quality proof or new TG expectation**.

**Capacity caution:** \`paperniuk/ds4\` README and \`docs/FLASH_NEXT_FORK.md\` remain current from Oct14 and explicitly publish IQ3_S ~51.0GiB resident weights; short prompt plain~26TG/MTP32-34TG; IQ3_S on M1Max64 **32K default**, ~56GiB planned @32K and ~60GiB @131K with MTP+vision; 262K for >=96GB. The prior P51 state records author's full262K formula **55.2GiB base +33KiB/token +2GiB reserve ≈65.9GiB**. Twelve FP16 QSA layers ×2 KV heads ×256 dims × K/V ×2B ×262144=**6.0GiB raw attention KV**. Q8_0 practical cross-runtime ~**3.19GiB**, KVarN K4V3 ideal packed **1.3125GiB** plus real group scales, alignment, pending high-precision tail, draft/indexer/page/workspace. A comparable KVarN implementation's quality-tested Qwen3.6-27B 64K / 1024-tail profile is **27.0% BF16 bytes**, suggesting ~1.6GiB at 262K before DS4-specific overhead, ~4.4GiB saved versus FP16. Thus a simple extrapolation 65.9−~4.4=**~61.5GiB**, still perilously close to OS, Metal wired and active-agent overhead. Even a ~60GiB process may be unsafe, and capacity is **not proven**. Avoid the misleading conclusion that KV-only compression guarantees full-context IQ3_S. Removing vision is useful for initial admission; turning off MTP is *diagnostic only*, not an acceptable permanent performance optimization. Deflate oversized QSA/indexer allocations where safe; leave correct structural state untouched. A small SSD expert spill might supply the last several GiB more safely than streaming random QSA reads. The source fork currently supports SSD expert streaming on DeepSeek/GLM Metal, **not Qwen4Exp**, and its disk KV cache is *inactive state checkpointing*, not active disk KV spill; these are separate unimplemented ports.

**K4V3 quality transfer caution (RECOVERED OLDER evidence, not newly measured):** \`Anbeeld/beellama.cpp\` README on **Qwen3.6-27B Q5_K_S, RTX3090, 64K Wikitext2**, 1024 tail reports **Q8/Q8 median KL .000897, p99.9 .087699, 55.5% BF16** versus **KVarN4/3 median .001112, p99.9 .113968, 27.0% BF16**. It is impressively near on median, but the upper tail is materially worse and this is NOT Flash-Next QSA nor xhigh/AA agent certification; do not claim parity. K4V2 saves only an *ideal* ~192MiB versus K4V3 at full262K (1bit of V ×12×2×256×262144); its quality risk likely dominates unless it crosses a measured capacity boundary. Earlier Qwen4Exp TurboQuant proof in state (starsder + thadreber-web) is a porting reference, not a DS4 native KVarN proof.
