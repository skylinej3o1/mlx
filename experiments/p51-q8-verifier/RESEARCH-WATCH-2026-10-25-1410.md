## Why only a few implementations, despite June2 paper and coding-agent acceleration?
- KVarN paper [arxiv 2606.03458](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.03458) created June2 (~4.5 months old); [vLLM RFC #46613](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/issues/46613) June24 still **OPEN**, Huawei vLLM fork working but full upstream adoption not proved. Nontrivial kernel and state logic: per-128 tile Hadamard, Sinkhorn/variance normalization, packing+scales, exact sink/recent tail, attention fused direct packed reads, rollback, MTP/GDN/QSA indexer. Higher complexity and test matrix even with agents.
- **Concrete negative datapoint** [eplt/mlx-kvarn](https://github.com/eplt/mlx-kvarn/blob/main/TECHNICAL_REPORT.md) June11 MLX-native simple dense Qwen2.5-1.5B K4V2 achieves cache compression **3.3×** but decode **121 vs FP16 170 tok/s = 29% slowdown** despite optimization from initial1 tok/s. Extra Hadamard Q/output and FP16 materialization per decode, fused Metal prototype 38% slower/buggy on multiheads. GSM8K Qwen2.5-3B n200 68.5% vs FP1666.5% within noise. Thus working compressed memory ≠ competitive native attention performance.
- **Concrete positive counterpoint** [Huawei RFC #46613](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/issues/46613) developer-reported *aggregate high-concurrency* throughputs Qwen3-14B FP16 475.7 vs K4V2 520.4 and GLM4.5-Air 258.9→304.5, not single M1 decode or original QFN. Benefits depend on being KV capacity/bandwidth limited, often less payoff in sparse/hybrid/QSA and on single-agent workload. Developers may reasonably prefer built-in INT8/FP8, K8V4 or q4 formats with limited port surface.
- GPT-class coding agents can plausibly build prototype in days/weeks, yet can't certify correctness or optimize GPU scheduling from prompt alone: specialized Metal kernels + live full262K memory trace + source-like long-agent reasoning, speculative rollback, state restore, concurrency. Not evidence of impossibility; absence of popular release alone doesn't prove fundamental flaw, but **29% real slowdown warrants skepticism**.
- Fixed Mac feasibility estimate original Paperniuk IQ3_S full262K planner ~55.2GiB base+8.69GiB growing+2GiB reserve=65.9GiB (planner not physical peak). Quantize only truly quantizable QSA/KV growing buffers. Zero assumptions of ideal 78% across everything. Metal headless/wired cap frees cap not RAM.
