Versus BF16 baseline, twelve-rep rotated model accuracy roughly **96.13 vs BF16 96.34%**, marginal p=0.057 and length ~2.6% longer within predeclared ±5% equivalence margin. **GPQA-D** five reps RTN~92.4% vs rotated~93.1%; 19 fixed/12 broken (p=0.28, NOT significant), rotated outputs **9.6% shorter than RTN**; largely recovers quant-induced extra reasoning. Performance caution: **unfused PyTorch FP32 rotation costs -12.4% throughput at c=4 and -6.0–6.2% at c=64/256** on specific TP8 hardware; follow-up [#63272](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/63272) reported fusion into activation quant is within ~1% of RTN but is a *separate unmerged/independently status-unqualified path*.
